More about us

Haveli Restaurant Mumbai is a place built around the simple idea that good food brings people together. Whether you're stopping in for a midweek dinner or planning something special, the room is set up to make you feel at home.

The kitchen draws on classic technique and seasonal produce, with a menu that changes as ingredients come into their best moment. Pair that with a thoughtful drinks list and friendly service, and you have the kind of place you'll want to return to.

We welcome solo diners, couples, families, and groups. If you have an allergy, a craving, or a celebration in mind, just tell us — we'll do our best to make it work.
